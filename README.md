# Firm Valuation When AI Shapes the Business Model

This repository contains the publication artifacts for:

> Walter Kurz, Wojtek Stricker, Stefan Marx, Frank Reinhardt, and Florian Kollberg. “Firm Valuation When AI Shapes the Business Model: A Milestone-Based Real-Options Framework for the AI Valuation Uncertainty Problem.” *Swissi AI Journal*, Volume 2026, Article SAIJ-f3jtignfyge3.

- Journal record: https://journal.swissi-ai.institute/en/doi/f3jtignfyge3
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21901247
- Full paper: [`paper.pdf`](paper.pdf)
- arXiv source archive: [`arxiv-source.zip`](arxiv-source.zip)
- Extracted LaTeX source: [`source/`](source/)

## Research artifacts

The paper develops a quantitative firm-valuation methodology that combines a milestone-gated real-options overlay, an AI-integration taxonomy, and AHP-based readiness assessment. Its worked single-firm case demonstrates the protocol and scenario logic. The complete model specification, case assumptions, calculations, tables, figures, bibliography, and declarations are preserved in the LaTeX source.

## Build

A TeX installation with `pdflatex` is required. Run:

```sh
./build.sh
```

The script compiles the paper from the committed source and bibliography output.
